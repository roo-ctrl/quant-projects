Quant Projects

Lahiru Fernando · personal articles where I took the theory from my University of Washington M.S. in Computational Finance and Risk Management and tried it on real market data.

  • Cointegration and the Two-Step Method (CFRM 502, 2023)
    Screens every S&P 500 pair for cointegration (Engle-Granger, Phillips-Ouliaris, KPSS) and tracks the surviving spreads out of sample.
  • Risk Measures: Mean-Variance vs. MAD vs. CVaR (CFRM 503, 2023)
    Three convex portfolio optimizers (CVXPY) over sector ETFs, rebalanced monthly and backtested 2018–2020 on returns, drawdown, and 5% CVaR.
  • Monte Carlo: Laplace vs. Normal Returns (CFRM 501, 2023)
    Fits a fat-tailed Laplace distribution by method of moments and compares its 1–21 day S&P 500 price forecasts against a Normal model, by sector.
  • Composition Monte Carlo With Earnings (CFRM 505, 2024)
    Mixes post-earnings price-move distributions for EPS beats and misses to forecast earnings-day ranges for JPM, C, and BAC.
